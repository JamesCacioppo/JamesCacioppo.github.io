My current bow is a **[Hoyt Alpha AX-2 32](https://s3.hoyt.com/uploads/2025/2025_Hunting_Whitepaper_FINAL.pdf)**. I bought it at [Double X Archery](https://doublexarchery.com/) in Leesburg, Virginia. I'm running a **28 1/4-inch draw length** and **60-pound draw weight**.

I put this bow together for whitetail hunting on the east coast and mid-west, and for Total Archery Challenge (TAC) events.

## Why I chose the Hoyt

What I did have was the research I'd done before going in. Hoyt's reputation for durability stood out to me, and that's ultimately why I chose it.

One detail that stuck with me was the dry-fire testing. In its [2025 hunting bow whitepaper](https://s3.hoyt.com/uploads/2025/2025_Hunting_Whitepaper_FINAL.pdf), Hoyt describes a 1,500 dry-fire test before approving a bow design for production. The prototype is tested at 80 pounds and a 30-inch draw length, and the riser has to survive without cracking, breaking, or bending. Strings can fail and be replaced during the test. This is a factory design test, and Hoyt explicitly warns owners never to dry-fire their bows.

That level of testing gave me confidence in the purchase, especially when I didn't yet have enough shooting experience to make the finer comparisons myself.

## The Spot Hogg Boonie single-pin sight

My sight is a **[Spot Hogg Boonie](https://spot-hogg.com/boonie/)** with a single pin. Spot Hogg's reputation for making a bulletproof sight was a big part of the appeal.

Double X helped me choose the sight and rest around my budget. They also cut me some discounts to keep the setup under my limit, which I appreciated.

The Boonie has first-, second-, and third-axis adjustments, and I can dial it for different distances. It also gives me room to change the pin configuration later. Spot Hogg offers [interchangeable pin inserts](https://spot-hogg.com/boonie-dt-single-pin-roti/), so I have options if I decide I want more pins in the future.

For now, I like the single pin a lot. No distractions. For what I'm doing, TAC events and whitetail hunting, it's the setup I want to use.

## The QAD rest and CBE quiver

The rest is a **[QAD Integrate R2](https://qadinc.com/ultrarest-integrate-r2/)**. This was the shop's recommendation based on my budget. I didn't go in with a strong preference for a particular rest; they helped pick one that fit the setup and the amount I wanted to spend.

I also have a **[CBE quiver](https://scottarchery.com/collections/custom-bow-equipment)**. It works fine. I don't have a long list of observations about that piece of equipment, which is probably a good thing.

## Two stabilizers for different situations

The shop also helped pick my **[CBE Torx Micro 10-inch stabilizer](https://scottarchery.com/products/torx-carbon-micro-stabilizer)** within the budget.

When I'm in the tree, though, I use the **[Hoyt Short Stop 2.0](https://lancasterarchery.com/products/hoyt-short-stop-2-0-stabilizer)** that came with the bow. So the stabilizer on the bow depends on where I'm shooting it.

## Moving from an index release to a thumb button

I started with a **[Scott Shark RTS](https://scottarchery.com/products/shark-rts)**, a wrist-strap release operated with the index finger. It was okay for learning, but I struggled to get a consistent feel when trying to use back tension to pull through the shot.

The amount I needed to pull seemed to depend on how much I'd already preloaded the trigger. I couldn't tell how much pressure I had on it because I wasn't getting the feedback I needed. That made the shot feel inconsistent to me.

I wanted to try a thumb-button release and initially planned to compare the **[STAN OnneX Thumb](https://stanoutdoors.com/products/solex-index-copy)** with the **[UV Button 2](https://ultraviewarchery.com/products/uv-button%E2%84%A2-2-copy)**. After trying the UV Button 2, though, I'm pretty settled. I haven't felt the need to continue that comparison.

What I like is the crisp, repeatable break. I'm originally a firearms guy, and the Button 2 reminds me of a good-quality single-stage trigger. That's the closest comparison I can make to how it feels, and it's what has made me want to stick with it.
